Before you begin

A few things to know

You will work through 6 practical scenarios. Some ask you to write or rewrite something, some ask you to make a choice, some ask you to review content. Go with your natural instinct — there are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in how you think, not whether you get things "correct".

Judgment calls

  • 1For writing scenarios, type the actual text you would use — not a description of it.
  • 2For review scenarios, mark whatever stands out to you — there is no fixed list of issues.
  • 3For judgment scenarios, go with your instinct — your honest first reaction is what matters most.
